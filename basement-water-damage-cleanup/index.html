Emergency basement cleanup

When a basement floods in the middle of the night

Emergency basement water damage cleanup in Piscataway starts by getting water out and keeping people safe. We answer 24/7 and dispatch the same day when crews are available, including nights, weekends and storm days.

Do not go downstairs if water is near the panel, a furnace or a water heater. Turn off power from a dry panel only if you can reach it safely, and never wade through water that may be electrified. Move what you can from stairs and upper shelves, and call the utility for any gas smell.

Why Piscataway basements take on water

Heavy rain, rising groundwater and brook overflow all push water toward below-grade rooms. Ambrose Brook flooded in 2018, and Ida on September 1 and 2, 2021 put water on River Road. Even a block away from the river, saturated soil can raise pressure against a foundation. That is why we ask whether water came through a crack, up through the floor or through a drain.

Where the source is a failed sump or a burst supply line, repair the source first. Cleanup without a fix is just a delay.

Water at the wall-floor joint

Seepage along the cove joint points to hydrostatic pressure and saturated soil.

A dark tide line on drywall

Finished basements wick water up the wall, so the real wet zone is higher than the stain.

Musty air

A basement that smells earthy is holding moisture, often in carpet, padding or insulation.

Efflorescence

White crusty powder on masonry means water has moved through the block and deposited minerals.

The cleanup in order

1

Pump and extract

Submersible pumps clear depth and wand units remove remaining water from floors.

2

Remove wet materials

Carpet, padding and the lowest drywall are removed if they cannot be dried safely.

3

Dry and dehumidify

LGR dehumidifiers and air movers run while moisture meters track the slab, studs and block.

4

Clean and treat

Surfaces are cleaned and treated, and we advise on mold risk and next steps.

Fast action prevents mold, which can begin within a day or two in humid conditions. If moisture lingered, our mold remediation service covers containment and removal. For a basement that took in contaminated water, the process matches flood damage cleanup.

What to protect and what to leave

In the first hours, the goal is to protect what can still be saved. Items stored on the floor, such as cardboard boxes and fabric, wick water quickly. Shelving and plastic bins keep contents out of the water.

Raise stored items

Move boxes onto shelves or tables if you can do it safely.

Stay off wet stairs

Water makes stairs slick, and wet carpet on steps hides hazards.

Leave appliances alone

A flooded furnace or water heater needs a technician before power returns.

Note the water line

A photo of the line on the wall helps scope and any claim.

Basements near the Raritan Valley and brooks like Ambrose Brook often see repeat water. After cleanup, ask a waterproofing contractor about grading, gutters, sump capacity and window wells, since these cause many repeat events.

What to do before a crew gets to the basement

Stay out of deep water

Standing water near the panel, outlets or a running sump pump can energize the water. Do not wade in.

Know what is in the water

Water from a sewer line or a river carries contamination. Keep children and pets out and wear boots and gloves if you must enter.

Do not run a household vacuum

A standard vacuum is not meant for water and creates a shock risk.

Move what you can upstairs

Lift furniture and boxes out of the wet area only if the path is dry.
