How the services fit together

Most jobs involve more than one service. A burst pipe begins with extraction, moves to structural drying and ends with drywall and flooring repair. A sewage backup adds contaminated-water protocol and disinfecting. A storm adds a roof or window opening that needs a roofer. We explain the sequence on every call, so you know what comes next.

The homepage covers water damage restoration in Piscataway as a whole, and the service pages go deeper on each cause. Standards matter here. We follow ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage restoration and ANSI/IICRC S520 where mold is involved.

What we do not do

We dry and restore buildings. We do not repair roofs, replace plumbing fixtures, service HVAC equipment or restore electronics and documents. Those need a roofer, a plumber, an HVAC technician or a specialist. When your problem needs one of them, we tell you plainly and describe what we found so they can act on it.

If water is spreading, call for water extraction or the service that matches the source, such as burst pipe or storm water damage. The dispatcher will sort out the rest.
