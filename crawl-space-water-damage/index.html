Crawl space damage

Water in your crawl space is not just a dirty floor

Crawl space water damage in Piscataway matters because the space sits directly under your living area. Moisture that collects there rises into floor joists, subfloor and insulation, and the air from the crawl space moves up into the house. A wet crawl space can lead to musty odors, sagging insulation and wood rot.

Our line answers 24/7, and we dispatch the same day when available. If you see standing water, stay out of the space if wiring or ductwork is wet, and call us.

Why crawl spaces get wet here

Heavy rain and high groundwater are the usual drivers. Ambrose Brook flooded in 2018, and Ida on September 1 and 2, 2021 left standing water in low areas of the township. In a crawl space with bare soil and no vapor barrier, groundwater and humidity move in easily. A burst supply line or failed drain can also drop water straight onto the dirt.

Not every Piscataway home has a crawl space. Basements and slabs are common too. If you are unsure what you have, a technician can identify it on a visit.

Standing water or wet soil

Pooling after rain shows a drainage or groundwater problem.

Sagging or damp insulation

Wet fiberglass falls away from the subfloor and stops working.

Musty smell upstairs

Air from a damp crawl space reaches living areas.

Dark growth on joists

Wood with surface growth has been damp for some time.

A crawl space moisture checklist

You can learn a lot about a crawl space from the hatch. Look for a musty smell, damp or bowed insulation, rusty fasteners and a dirt floor that is dark or wet. Take photos from the opening, since climbing in is not always safe with wet wiring and low clearance.

Look for standing water

Shine a flashlight from the hatch and note depth and where it collects.

Check the vapor barrier

Plastic sheeting on the ground should be intact and lapped at seams.

Inspect the insulation

Insulation hanging down means it has absorbed moisture.

Smell the air

A strong earthy odor often means long-term dampness.

Crawl spaces under additions and older Piscataway homes may have little ventilation, which traps humid summer air. A dehumidifier and a sealed barrier often work better than extra vents, but that is a decision for a crawl space contractor after the water is cleaned up.

Standards and scope

Our drying plan follows ANSI/IICRC S500. When mold is present, the process follows ANSI/IICRC S520. Neither standard replaces the work of a waterproofing contractor, who addresses drainage and grading around the foundation.

What to do before help arrives: crawl space

Do not enter a flooded crawl space

Standing water, low clearance and wiring make it unsafe to crawl into on your own.

Check from the access hatch

Look in with a flashlight and note depth, color and any smell.

Shut off a leaking supply

If a pipe is spraying, close the main. Leave the repair to a plumber.

Look for sump pump power

If a pump runs on an extension cord near water, leave it alone and call.
