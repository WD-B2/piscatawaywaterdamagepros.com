Privacy Policy

A short, plain statement of what we collect and what we do with it.

24/7 emergency line(732) 352-9660 Call now

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Piscataway Water Damage Pros, at 1370 Centennial Ave, Piscataway, NJ 08854, handles information when you visit this website or call our line. It was last updated on November 13, 2026.

Information you give us

This site does not use contact forms. If you call, we collect what you tell us, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the water damage, so we can dispatch a crew and do the work. If you email us, we receive your email address and the message.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the hosting service may log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages visited. We use it to keep the site secure and working. We do not sell this information.

How we use and share information

  • To dispatch crews, perform work and follow up.
  • To prepare documents your insurer may request, such as photos and moisture logs, when you ask us to.
  • To meet legal obligations.
  • We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with subcontractors or service providers who help us do the work, and only as needed for that purpose.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email contact@piscatawaywaterdamagepros.com or call (732) 352-9660.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Changes

We may update this policy, and the date at the top will change when we do.
