When floodwater is entering your Piscataway home

Emergency flood damage cleanup in Piscataway starts with safety and speed. The line is open 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, and crews go out the same day when they are available. Flood damage cleanup means pumping out floodwater and silt, drying the structure, and sanitizing every surface the water reached.

While you wait, keep people and pets out of the water, lift valuables to a dry level, and shut off the main water valve if a pipe is involved. Cut power at the panel only if you can reach it without standing in water. For any gas smell or sparking outlet, call the utility or 911 first, then call us.

Why Piscataway floods the way it does

The township sits on the south side of the Raritan Valley, and river water has reached homes more than once. During Ida on September 1 and 2, 2021, River Road and parts of the Raritan River corridor flooded, and Middlesex County received a federal disaster declaration. The river crested again near Birchview Gardens on January 10, 2024.

Brook flooding is a separate pattern. Ambrose Brook overflowed in 2018, and water backed into low yards well away from the river. The practical point is that river and brook water is not clean. It carries silt, sewage and runoff, so we treat it as contaminated and remove porous materials that cannot be disinfected.

Rutgers' Livingston and Busch campuses, the Raritan Landing area and the older streets around Possumtown and New Market all have different drainage. We ask where the water came from and how long it stood, because that decides what can be saved.

Signs the damage is bigger than the water you can see

Water lines on walls

A tide mark on drywall shows how high the water stood. Drywall wicks moisture above that line, so the real wet zone is taller than the stain.

Silt film on floors

A gray or brown film after the water recedes means river or brook sediment. It holds bacteria and must be washed off before drying.

Swollen trim and doors

Baseboards that cup and doors that stick mean wood has absorbed water and will keep moving until it is dried.

A damp, sour smell

Odor within a day or two usually means wet insulation, padding or the cavity behind a wall. Surface cleaning will not fix it.

How flood damage cleanup works, step by step

We follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage restoration standard, which treats flood water as Category 3. That category drives the plan: contaminated materials come out, and everything that stays gets cleaned and dried to a measured target.

1

Remove the water

Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water. Silt is shoveled and rinsed before it dries into a crust.

2

Strip what cannot be saved

Wet carpet, padding and the lower section of drywall are cut out and bagged, and insulation is pulled.

3

Clean and sanitize

Hard surfaces and framing are washed and treated. Contents are sorted into clean, restorable and discard.

4

Dry to a number

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run while moisture meters and thermal imaging confirm the framing is dry.
