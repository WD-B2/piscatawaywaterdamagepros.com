The fastest way to reach us is by phone. The 24/7 line is staffed by a dispatcher who can book a crew, answer questions and talk you through the first steps. We do not use forms, because water damage cannot wait for an email reply.

Call (732) 352-9660 and tell the dispatcher your address, where the water is and what you have already shut off. Same-day response is available when crews are free.
