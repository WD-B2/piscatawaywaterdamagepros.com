Emergency burst pipe response

When a pipe bursts: the first 15 minutes

Emergency burst pipe water damage cleanup in Piscataway starts with the main water valve. Shut it off first, then open a faucet low in the house to drain what is left. Our line is open 24/7 and crews dispatch the same day when available, including nights, weekends and holidays.

Turn off power at the panel only if you can reach it without standing in water. Move valuables up, photograph the damage and call us. A plumber will fix the pipe, and we will handle the water.

Why pipes fail here

Piscataway's water supply comes mainly from New Jersey American Water, and most pipe failures are inside the home rather than on the street. Freeze and thaw cycles, corrosion in older copper and galvanized lines, and loose fittings all cause bursts. A main in the street can fail too.

One 48-inch main on Possumtown Road leaked on August 2, 2021 and led to a boil-water advisory. That kind of event is a utility repair, while a pipe inside your wall is yours.

Pressure changes during utility work can stress older fittings. New Jersey American Water began a $2 million upgrade on March 26, 2026. After any local pressure change or flushing, check exposed pipes and fixtures for new drips.

A sudden drop in water pressure

A burst line can drain pressure to the fixtures downstream.

Sound of running water with taps off

A hissing or rushing sound in a wall points to an active leak.

Warm spots on a floor

A hot water line leak under a slab or floor can heat the surface.

A spike in the water bill

A hidden leak can run for days before a bill reveals it.

The cleanup process

1

Extract

Standing water is removed with pumps and wand extractors.

2

Locate the path

Moisture meters and thermal imaging show water inside walls, ceilings and floors.

3

Open and dry

Wet drywall and insulation are removed as needed, and air movers and dehumidifiers dry the rest.

4

Repair

After the plumber fixes the pipe, we rebuild what was opened.

Clean supply water is usually Category 1 at first. Under ANSI/IICRC S500, it moves toward Category 2 if it sits, which is why a quick response keeps the job smaller.

After the plumber leaves

Once the pipe is repaired, the focus shifts to proof of dryness. Plumbers do not measure wall moisture, so ask for readings from the restoration crew. A wall that looks fine but reads wet will cause trouble later.

Keep the broken section

A sample of the failed pipe helps with claims.

Ask for a moisture log

Daily readings show drying progress.

Check below and beside

Look at ceilings and neighboring walls for staining.

Test fixtures

Run taps and check connections for new drips.

What to do in the first ten minutes

Close the main shutoff

Turn it clockwise until it stops. Open a low faucet afterward to drain the line.

Cut power to wet areas

If water is near outlets or a ceiling light, shut off those circuits only from a dry spot.

Stay out from under a bulging ceiling

Water can pool above drywall and let go all at once. Keep clear and tell the dispatcher what you see.

Photograph before cleanup

Wide and close photos help any later claim.
