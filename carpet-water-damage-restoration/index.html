Carpet decisions

Soaked carpet: save it or replace it?

Carpet water damage in Piscataway is about timing and water source. Clean water on carpet that has been wet for a day or less can often be extracted and dried in place. Contaminated water or a soaking that lasts several days usually means the carpet and padding come out.

Our line answers 24/7, and we dispatch the same day when available. Until we arrive, lift furniture off wet carpet, blot standing water and keep foot traffic off the area.

Why padding decides the outcome

Padding is a foam sponge that sits under carpet and holds water far longer than the surface does. It dries slowly and, once contaminated, cannot be sanitized. In most water losses, padding is removed and replaced even when the carpet is saved. That is why a surface that feels dry can still sit on wet padding.

Older Piscataway homes near Possumtown and Randolphville may have carpet over wood subfloor, which adds another moisture layer. We check the subfloor with a moisture meter before deciding.

Carpet and padding: what homeowners can do in the first hour

A short list of moves in the first hour makes a big difference. Remove everything that can wick water off the carpet, such as boxes, magazines and cardboard, and lift furniture legs onto foil or plastic so wood legs do not stain the carpet.

Blot, do not rub

Press towels into wet carpet to lift water without spreading the stain.

Lift furniture

Place foil or plastic blocks under legs to prevent staining.

Ventilate carefully

Run the HVAC fan and keep windows closed if outdoor air is humid.

Photograph the room

Capture wide and close views for any claim.

Carpet in basements and ground-floor rooms near the Raritan Valley floodplain faces the highest risk, since groundwater and storm water arrive without warning. If the water came from outside, plan for carpet and padding removal, because outside water is contaminated.

Why the tack strip matters

Tack strips along the walls hold carpet in place and wick water into the wall base. We check them and the baseboard behind, since staining there tells us how far moisture traveled.

Why the water category decides the carpet

Under ANSI/IICRC S500, the water category drives the carpet decision. Carpet and cushion that took clean supply water can often be extracted and dried in place. Carpet that took sewage or floodwater is contaminated, and the standard treats that porous material as something to remove. Asking which category you have is the fastest way to know if your carpet is a candidate for saving.
