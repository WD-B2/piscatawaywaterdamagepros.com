Symptom check

Musty smell or dark spots on a wall?

If you see dark growth on a wall, smell something earthy in a closet or notice speckling on a basement ceiling, mold may have grown after a past leak. Mold remediation in Piscataway is the work of removing that growth safely and correcting the moisture that fed it.

Mold remediation is the trade name for containment, removal and cleaning of mold-affected materials. Our line answers 24/7 and we dispatch when available.

Why mold appears after water damage

Mold needs moisture, a food source and time. Wet drywall, wood and carpet padding provide food, and growth can begin within a day or two when humidity stays high. Piscataway summers and wet seasons keep basements and crawl spaces humid, which is why damp areas are often where growth appears.

We do not make health claims about mold exposure. If you are concerned about symptoms, speak with a physician.

How remediation is done

We follow the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard for mold remediation. The core idea is to contain the area, control airborne spores, remove contaminated porous materials and clean what remains, then fix the moisture source.

1

Assess and contain

We map the affected area and set containment with negative air where appropriate.

2

Remove

Porous contaminated materials are bagged and removed.

3

Clean

Surfaces are HEPA vacuumed and cleaned.

4

Dry and prevent

Dehumidification and moisture control address the cause.

Killing mold with a spray does not fix a wet wall. Without drying and removal, growth returns.

What to do and what to avoid

When you find mold, the instinct is to scrub it. Dry brushing or sanding can release spores into the air and spread them. A better first move is to leave the area alone, reduce moisture if you can, and call for an assessment.

Fix the water first

Stop the leak or dry the area, because growth returns where moisture stays.

Do not disturb it

Avoid sanding, scraping or blowing air across growth.

Limit foot traffic

Close the door and keep the HVAC from pulling air from the room.

Document the area

Photograph it with a ruler and the date, which helps scope and any claim.

Basements are the most common site we see, followed by bathrooms and crawl spaces. Basements here stay humid through summer, and a past leak can leave drywall damp enough for growth to start behind paint. Visible spots are often the edge of a larger area behind the wall.

Honest limits

We do not make claims about how mold affects health. If someone in the home has symptoms, a physician is the right contact, and the home should be dried and cleaned regardless.
